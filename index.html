03 — Closed-Loop Instrumentation

Hydrostatic Pressure Test Rig with PID Control

DEPLOYED

Closed-loop hydrostatic pressure test rig with PID-controlled electropneumatic regulator and 6-channel 24-bit strain measurement. Applied 0–22 MPa across two pressure sequences to characterise Young's modulus of an aluminium sample — result: 70.8 GPa (2.65% error vs textbook). Data logged as JSON via ESP32-S3 and post-processed in Python. PCB in IP-rated steel enclosure with shielded cabling.
